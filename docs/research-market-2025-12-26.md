## 8. Key Success Factors

Based on market research, critical success factors are:

1. **Visual UX** - Pie charts and allocation % are table stakes
2. **Mobile-first** - Gen Z/Millennials prefer app-based investing
3. **Passive-focused** - ETF/index fund strategies dominate
4. **International** - US market is saturated; global is opportunity
5. **Trust** - Edit/delete capabilities build trust (from competitive research)
6. **Low/Free Pricing** - 46% don't invest due to lack of money
