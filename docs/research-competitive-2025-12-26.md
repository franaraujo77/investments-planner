## Executive Summary

This competitive intelligence report analyzes how competitors in the portfolio management and investment tracking space handle the key pain points identified in our brainstorming session: portfolio CRUD operations, visual allocation feedback, onboarding experiences, and internationalization.

### Key Competitive Insights

1. **M1 Finance's "Pie" System** is the gold standard for visual portfolio allocation - intuitive pie charts with real-time percentage tracking
2. **Full CRUD is table stakes** - All major competitors allow complete portfolio editing and deletion
3. **Visual feedback is expected** - Pie charts, allocation percentages, and "remaining %" are standard features
4. **Multi-currency support is a differentiator** - Kubera, Sharesight, and Portseido excel for international users
5. **Wizard-style onboarding** is considered best practice for reducing drop-off

## 2. Competitor Analysis

### Tier 1: Primary Competitors (Direct Competition)

#### M1 Finance

**Type:** Robo-Advisor + DIY | **Price:** Free (0 fees)

**Portfolio Management:**

- ✅ Full CRUD: Add, edit, delete slices at any time
- ✅ Two deletion methods: (1) Set to 0% for reinvestment, (2) Sell first for cash
- ✅ Up to 100 slices per pie, 5 pies per account
- ✅ Nested pies (pies within pies)

**Visual Feedback:**

- ✅ **"Pie" visualization** - Signature circle graph interface
- ✅ Target percentages must equal 100% (enforced)
- ✅ Performance shown visually - outperforming slices grow away from center
- ✅ Dynamic rebalancing shows allocation drift

**Key Quote:** _"Total target percentages for all Slices must equal 100%. Each Slice must be set to at least 1%."_

**Sources:** [M1 Help Center - Edit Portfolio](https://help.m1.com/en/articles/9332119-edit-my-m1-portfolio), [What is a Pie](https://m1.com/invest/what-is-a-pie/)

#### Empower (formerly Personal Capital)

**Type:** Portfolio Tracker | **Price:** Free

**Portfolio Management:**

- ✅ Aggregates from all accounts (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, etc.)
- ✅ View-only tracking (doesn't manage actual investments)
- ✅ Connect unlimited accounts

**Visual Feedback:**

- ✅ **Interactive colored rectangles** showing asset class percentages
- ✅ Click to zoom in/out on allocation categories
- ✅ Breakdown by: industry, market cap, stock/bond allocation
- ✅ Investment Checkup across 12 dimensions
- ✅ Efficient Frontier curve comparison
- ✅ **Smart Weighting™** recommended allocation comparison

**Key Quote:** _"Empower pulls data from all of your brokerage accounts and gives you a full breakdown of your allocation. You can click on one of the boxes, and it zooms in and out on each."_

**Sources:** [Empower Portfolio Analysis](https://www.empower.com/tools/portfolio-analysis), [Rob Berger Review](https://robberger.com/empower-review/)

## 6. Strategic Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (Match Table Stakes)

1. **Implement Edit/Delete Portfolio**
   - M1 Finance approach: Full CRUD with confirmation dialogs
   - This is table stakes - every competitor has this

2. **Add Pie Chart Visualization**
   - Copy M1's "Pie" concept for strategy view
   - Show asset class distribution visually

3. **Add Live Allocation % Display**
   - Show running total: "75% allocated, 25% remaining"
   - Enforce 100% rule like M1 Finance

### Medium-Term Differentiation

4. **International Support (i18n)**
   - Add regional settings (decimal separator)
   - This is where you can beat M1 Finance (US-only)

5. **Wizard-Style Onboarding**
   - Best practice from fintech: progressive disclosure
   - Reduce cognitive load, increase completion

### Long-Term Opportunities

6. **Brokerage API/Statement Import**
   - Reduce manual data entry
   - Match Sharesight, Empower capabilities
